## Existing DC Untold Thesis

DC Untold is not just a facts channel. The existing playbook frames it as a narrative claim under Unofficial DC:

- The real history of DC that did not make the monuments.
- Hidden neighborhoods, people, culture, music, erasure, and resilience.
- Episode 1: Swampoodle, the neighborhood under Union Station.
- Tone: truth told with love, revelation rather than lecture.
- Podcast path: NotebookLM source-gathering, Wondercraft polish, YouTube as evergreen home, Spotify/Apple as audio distribution.

## Recommendation

Build DC Untold as a daily short-form history feed first, then roll the strongest facts into longer podcast episodes.

The daily unit should be small enough to repeat every day:

1. One verified DC fact.
2. One emotional or surprising hook.
3. One short explanation of why it matters.
4. One source set.
5. One visual direction.
6. Three publishing variants: Instagram/Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn.
7. One future podcast bucket.

## Content Object Shape

Use this schema for a Notion database or a local CSV/JSON queue.

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `Date` | Target publish date |
| `Fact Title` | Short internal name |
| `Hook` | First line for social/video |
| `Verified Fact` | The factual core, written plainly |
| `Why It Matters` | The DC Untold interpretation |
| `Location` | Neighborhood/site when applicable |
| `Era` | Time period |
| `Sources` | URLs or citations |
| `Source Confidence` | High / Medium / Needs review |
| `IG Caption` | Feed/Reels caption |
| `Shorts Script` | 20-45 second spoken/onscreen script |
| `LinkedIn Copy` | More reflective professional variant |
| `Visual Brief` | Background, archival image, map, animation idea |
| `Podcast Bucket` | Episode theme it may roll into later |
| `Status` | Idea / Researched / Scripted / Visual Ready / Scheduled / Posted |
| `Posted URLs` | Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn links |

## Pilot Cadence

- Daily IG/Reels/Shorts: one fact per day, 20-45 seconds.
- LinkedIn business page: 3-4 strongest posts per week, not necessarily every daily fact.
- Kevin personal LinkedIn: selective commentary or reposts when the fact connects to leadership, culture, business, or civic memory.
- Podcast: collect 5-10 related daily facts into one episode cluster.

## Reference Channel Lessons

### Inside History

What to borrow:
- Theme-led packaging rather than random trivia.
- Email/subscription capture as the deeper relationship layer.
- Creator voice around curation: "closer to the past one theme at a time."

DC Untold adaptation:
- Weekly themes like "Neighborhoods erased," "Music the city made," "The land under the monuments," "Black DC institutions," and "DC before the rebrand."

### OverSimplified

What to borrow:
- Complex history becomes legible through a clean hook, simplified characters, and visual humor.
- Do not fear simplification as long as the sources are real.

DC Untold adaptation:
- Use simplification for the first 10 seconds, then add emotional depth. DC Untold should be less snarky and more soulful.

### Kings and Generals

What to borrow:
- Maps, movement, and spatial context make history feel understandable.
- Repeated visual grammar helps viewers know how to watch.

DC Untold adaptation:
- Use animated DC maps: neighborhood boundaries, demolition zones, redlining lines, streetcar routes, migration patterns, go-go venue clusters.

### Crash Course

What to borrow:
- Fast structure, witty host voice, named segments, and recurring visual devices.
- The educational spine is clear even when the pacing is playful.

DC Untold adaptation:
- Build a repeatable short structure: hook, setup, turn, meaning, source line.

### The History Guy

What to borrow:
- Forgotten history as the core lane.
- Consistent publishing cadence.
- A recognizable narrator/presenter point of view.

DC Untold adaptation:
- Make "the history that did not make the monument" the recurring promise.

### History Hit

What to borrow:
- A network mindset: video, podcast, articles, newsletter, behind-the-scenes material, and platform-native clips all feed one brand.
- Experts/sources elevate trust.

DC Untold adaptation:
- Daily facts become social posts, source notes become Open Brain entries, best clusters become podcast episodes, and strongest episodes become evergreen YouTube uploads.

## Pilot Format

### Instagram/Reels/YouTube Shorts

Length: 20-45 seconds.

Structure:
1. Hook: "There used to be a neighborhood under Union Station."
2. Setup: what/where/when.
3. Turn: the thing people do not know.
4. Meaning: why this still matters.
5. Close: "This is DC Untold."

Visual style:
- Archival image or map base when available.
- Slow but visible camera movement.
- Bold captions.
- Minimal logo bug.
- End card: `DC Untold | The history that did not make the monument`.

### LinkedIn Business Page

Use the Unofficial DC business page for the main DC Untold publishing lane. Keep it smart and culturally grounded, not too platform-casual.

Format:
- 600-1,200 characters.
- Strong first line.
- White space.
- One specific question at the end.
- Source/context in first comment or final sentence.

### Podcast Future

Do not wait for the podcast to start posting. Instead, tag each daily fact with a future episode bucket.

Example buckets:
- Swampoodle / Union Station / neighborhood erasure.
- Cherry blossoms and the tourist version of DC.
- Go-go and cultural preservation.
- Chocolate City and demographic memory.
- Don't Mute DC and modern civic identity.
