## Fact
In 1888, Washington schoolteacher and federal clerk Miriam Benjamin patented a 'gong and signal chair' letting a seated person summon help with a button, only the second patent issued to a Black American woman. The mechanism she pitched to Congress is a direct ancestor of today's airplane call button.

## Takeaway
A Black civil servant working in DC's segregated school system quietly designed a signaling system now used on every commercial flight.
