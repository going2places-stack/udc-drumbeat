# A Capitol Hill rowhouse became the first lesbian National Landmark

## Fact
Between 1971 and 1973, a modest rowhouse at 219 11th Street SE was home to twelve lesbian feminists known as the Furies Collective. Their writing shaped lesbian feminist separatism nationally, and members went on to found Olivia Records and the country's first independent feminist publisher, Diana Press.

## Takeaway
A small group living together on Capitol Hill produced ideas and institutions that reshaped feminist and lesbian culture across the entire country.
