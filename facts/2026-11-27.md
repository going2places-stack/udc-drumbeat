# AdMo's Party Streets Are Named for an Integration Pact

- Date: 2026-11-27
- Status: Posted
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1950s
- Place: Adams Morgan
- Tags: adams-morgan, school-integration, neighborhood-naming, civil-rights, 1950s, desegregation

## Fact
Washington's trendy Adams Morgan neighborhood isn't named for a founder or developer. It's named for two segregated DC elementary schools, white Adams and Black Morgan, whose principals built a joint 'Better Neighborhood Conference' in 1955 to peacefully desegregate their schools and the surrounding blocks.

## Takeaway
Every happy-hour sign in Adams Morgan carries the name of a grassroots school-integration effort, not a person.
