# DC's First Public Housing Was Designed by a Black Architect

## Fact
Langston Terrace Dwellings in Northeast DC was the first federally funded public housing complex in the city, built 1935-38 for Black families during segregation. It was designed by Hilyard Robinson, a Bauhaus-trained Black architect, and its courtyard features a terra-cotta frieze tracing African American history from slavery to migration.

## Takeaway
A Black architect used cutting-edge European modernism to build dignity into New Deal housing for families the government otherwise excluded.
