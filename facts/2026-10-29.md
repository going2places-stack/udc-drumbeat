# The 'Fifth' Brown Case Started at an Anacostia Schoolhouse Door

## Fact
In 1950, Black parents in Anacostia tried to enroll their children at the all-white John Philip Sousa Junior High and were turned away. Their case, Bolling v. Sharpe, became the lesser-known fifth case decided alongside Brown v. Board—but because DC isn't a state, the Supreme Court had to invent a new constitutional argument to desegregate it.

## Takeaway
DC's lack of statehood forced the Supreme Court to create an entirely separate legal doctrine just to desegregate its own schools.
