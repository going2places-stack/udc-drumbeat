# DC's First Black Theater Wasn't Black-Owned

- Date: 2026-11-23
- Status: Posted
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1910s
- Place: Shaw, NW Washington DC
- Tags: shaw, u-street, theater-history, segregation-era-business, black-entertainment, ownership-inequality

## Fact
When the Howard Theatre opened in Shaw in 1910 as the nation's first major theater built for Black audiences, it wasn't financed by the community it served. It was founded and owned by the white-run National Amusement Company, which profited for over a decade before Black lessees took over.

## Takeaway
Segregation created a captive Black entertainment market, and outside investors—not the community—were often the first to cash in on it.
