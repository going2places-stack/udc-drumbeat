# Congress Banned Wires, So DC Built Power Underground

- Date: 2026-10-21
- Status: Posted
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1888-1962
- Place: Georgetown / Downtown DC
- Tags: transit-history, civic-infrastructure, engineering, streetcars, georgetown, 19th-century

## Fact
In 1888, Congress banned overhead utility wires in central Washington to protect the capital's skyline. This forced engineers to build a unique underground electric conduit system that powered the city's streetcars instead of overhead trolley wires, lasting until 1962.

## Takeaway
A law about aesthetics forced DC to pioneer a transit technology so specialized almost no other city in the world used it.
