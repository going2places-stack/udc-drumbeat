## Fact
In 1984, developers razed an apartment building in Shepherd Park to build a Wendy's. The next morning, retired teacher Juanita E. Thornton began calling city officials, launching a "Books Not Burgers" campaign that replaced the fast-food plan with a public library—even though the Washington Post later reported she was legally blind and unable to read.

## Takeaway
A woman who couldn't read herself fought hardest to guarantee her neighborhood had a library, proving civic infrastructure is often won by people who'll never personally use it.
