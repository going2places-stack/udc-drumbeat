## Fact
In 1967 the Smithsonian didn't build a new wing on the Mall—it converted an old movie house, the Carver Theater on Nichols Avenue, into the Anacostia Neighborhood Museum, an experimental outreach project for a Black neighborhood many felt the Mall museums weren't built for.

## Takeaway
Decades before the National Museum of African American History and Culture, Washington's Black community already had its own Smithsonian museum, built with its own advisory council.
