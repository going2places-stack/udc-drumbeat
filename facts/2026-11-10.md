## Fact
The Maine Avenue Fish Market, America's oldest continuously operating open-air market, has sold Chesapeake Bay seafood from the same Southwest DC wharves since 1805. Those same sailboats that hauled fish up to Washington sometimes sailed back down the Bay carrying a very different kind of cargo: people escaping slavery.

## Takeaway
An ordinary commercial fish route doubled as a hidden lifeline out of slavery, hiding resistance in plain sight along DC's working waterfront.
