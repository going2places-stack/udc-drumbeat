# DC Firefighters Had to Segregate Themselves to Get Promoted

## Fact
Three Black DC firefighters who'd served since 1868 without ever being promoted to officer petitioned in 1918 to form their own, all-Black company. The city agreed, creating Engine Company No. 4 in 1919—finally giving its petitioners captain, lieutenant, and sergeant ranks, decades before the department desegregated in 1962.

## Takeaway
For Black civil servants in early-20th-century DC, building a segregated institution was sometimes the only route to the promotions integration had denied them.
