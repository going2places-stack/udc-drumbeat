# The Black 'Chevy Chase' Erased Before It Could Rise

- Date: 2026-10-20
- Status: Ready

- Era: 1906
- Place: Friendship Heights
- Tags: black-wealth, friendship-heights, lost-neighborhoods, real-estate, racial-exclusion

## Fact
In 1906, four Black entrepreneurs bought land in Friendship Heights to build an upscale Black suburb nicknamed 'a Chevy Chase for Black people,' signing deals with at least 28 homebuyers. The Chevy Chase Land Company refused to finalize the sales after white neighbors issued threats, and the community, called Belmont, never came to exist.

## Takeaway
A thriving Black-owned suburb was quietly killed by a land company's refusal to close the deal, showing how DC's wealth gap was built one blocked transaction at a time.
