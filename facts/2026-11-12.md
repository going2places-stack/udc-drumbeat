# DC's First Racially Mixed Jury Sat in Judgment in 1869

## Fact
In 1869, Black bank founder and inventor Leonard C. Bailey served on Washington, D.C.'s first racially integrated jury, six Black men and six white men, which tried and acquitted Millie Gaines of murdering her white lover.

## Takeaway
It shows Reconstruction-era DC briefly built real working multiracial institutions years before Jim Crow swept them away.
