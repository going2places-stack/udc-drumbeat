# DC's 1903 Hall Was Built, Financed & Designed by Black Washingtonians

- Date: 2026-10-12
- Status: Posted
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1900s
- Place: U Street Corridor, Shaw, NW
- Tags: u-street, black-history, architecture, duke-ellington, shaw, mutual-aid

## Fact
In 1903, U Street's True Reformers Hall became one of the first buildings in the U.S. fully designed, financed, and constructed by African Americans. The Washington Post was so struck by this that it ran a headline announcing the achievement. The hall later hosted a teenage Duke Ellington's first paid gig.

## Takeaway
It shows how Black Washingtonians built lasting civic infrastructure and culture under Jim Crow, decades before U Street became known as 'Black Broadway.'
