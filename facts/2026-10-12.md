# The Drinking Straw Was Invented—and Built—in DC

- Date: 2026-10-12

## Fact
In 1888, Marvin C. Stone, a Civil War veteran running a paper cigarette-holder business in Washington, patented the modern paper drinking straw. His company later built an automated manufacturing plant in Brookland, where straws were mass-produced for decades.

## Takeaway
A global everyday object traces its industrial birthplace to a modest NE DC neighborhood factory, not a big coastal city.
