# DC's Only Amusement Park Was Black-Built, Black-Owned

## Fact
From 1921 to 1940, Deanwood was home to Suburban Gardens, the only major amusement park ever built inside Washington, DC. It was developed by the Black-owned Universal Development and Loan Company, led by civil engineer Howard D. Woodson, after Glen Echo and other regional parks barred Black visitors.

## Takeaway
When segregation locked Black Washingtonians out of every amusement park in the region, Deanwood residents financed, designed, and ran their own.
