## Fact
In 1909, Nannie Helen Burroughs opened the National Training School for Women and Girls in Northeast DC—one of the few major Black schools of its era that deliberately turned down white financial backing, unlike contemporaries such as Tuskegee. It was funded, built, and run entirely by African Americans.

## Takeaway
It's a rare early model of Black educational self-determination, built without strings attached to white philanthropy.
