# Howard's TV Station Was the Nation's First Black-Owned PBS Outlet

## Fact
In 1980, Howard University signed on WHMM-TV (later WHUT), making it the country's first public television station owned and operated by a historically Black university. Decades later, Howard nearly sold off its broadcast license in a 2016 FCC spectrum auction before students, alumni, and faculty organized a #KeepWHUTblack campaign that stopped the sale.

## Takeaway
A Howard-run TV signal became the nation's last Black-owned public television station, and a grassroots campaign is the only reason it still broadcasts today.
