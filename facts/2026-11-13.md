# Swampoodle Built a Hospital in 8 Days to Save Its Church

- Date: 2026-11-13
- Status: Posted
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1860s-1900s
- Place: Swampoodle (now NoMa)
- Tags: irish-immigrants, swampoodle, civil-war, noma, union-station, catholic-church

## Fact
In 1862 the Union Army tried to seize Swampoodle's new St. Aloysius Church, the heart of DC's poor Irish immigrant enclave, to use as a Civil War hospital. Parishioners, many of them construction laborers, instead built an entire 250-bed hospital from scratch in eight days to keep their church standing.

## Takeaway
An immigrant neighborhood's labor and solidarity saved its church from the army, but decades later the same city razed the whole community anyway to build Union Station.
