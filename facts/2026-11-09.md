# DC's Opera Diva Performed La Traviata on a Potomac Barge

## Fact
In 1943, DC-born soprano Lillian Evanti — already famed as the first Black singer to join a major European opera company — starred as Violetta in the National Negro Opera Company's La Traviata, staged on a barge floating on the Potomac River because segregated venues shut the production out.

## Takeaway
Locked out of America's whites-only opera stages, Black Washington built its own grand stage — literally floating on the river.
