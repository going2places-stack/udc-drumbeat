# A Shaw Noise Complaint Made Go-Go DC's Official Sound

- Date: 2026-10-24
- Status: Ready
- Angle: DC Is More Than Politics
- Era: 2019-2020
- Place: Shaw (7th Street & Florida Ave NW)
- Tags: go-go-music, gentrification, black-culture, shaw, activism, music-history

## Fact
In 2019, a new luxury-condo resident near 7th and Florida Ave NW got a MetroPCS store's go-go speakers shut off after 24 years of play. The backlash, #DontMuteDC, drew support from over 80,000 people worldwide and led the DC Council to legally name go-go the city's official music in 2020.

## Takeaway
A fight over street-corner speaker volume became a legal statement that Black Washington's culture can't simply be priced or complained out of the city.
