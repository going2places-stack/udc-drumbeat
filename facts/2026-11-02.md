# The Howard Trustee Who Fenced Howard Out

- Date: 2026-11-02

## Fact
In 1873, Howard University trustee Amzi Barber developed LeDroit Park next door as a whites-only enclave, enforcing it with a literal perimeter fence and armed guards. Black Washingtonians tore the fence down in 1888, and by the early 1900s the neighborhood had flipped into the city's premier Black intellectual address.

## Takeaway
A university founder physically walled Black Washington out of his own development, and it took a street-level uprising to tear that barrier down.
