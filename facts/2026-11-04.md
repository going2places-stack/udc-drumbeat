# Barry Farm Teens Forced Open DC's Segregated Pools

- Date: 2026-11-04
- Status: Posted

- Era: 1940s-1950s
- Place: Barry Farm-Hillsdale, Anacostia, SE DC

## Fact
In 1949, teenagers from Barry Farm-Hillsdale—a community the Freedmen's Bureau had founded for formerly enslaved people in 1867—repeatedly tried to swim at the whites-only Anacostia Pool. The resulting clashes shut the pool down, and when it reopened in 1950, it was desegregated, helping force the desegregation of all of DC's public pools.

## Takeaway
A handful of teens from a historic freedpeople's settlement helped force open every segregated public pool in the nation's capital years before Brown v. Board.
