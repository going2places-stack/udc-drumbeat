# DC's Black Bank Kept Money in DC — Riggs Mostly Didn't

- Date: 2026-11-24
- Status: Ready
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1913-1970s
- Place: Shaw / U Street
- Tags: black-owned-business, banking, redlining, civil-rights, shaw, black-wealth

## Fact
In the early 1970s, Industrial Bank — the only Black-owned bank in DC when it opened in 1913 — kept 94% of its mortgage loans inside the District, while Riggs National Bank, the city's biggest lender, kept only 39%. Riggs and other white-owned banks lent mostly west of Rock Creek Park, while Industrial focused on Black neighborhoods.

## Takeaway
A single Black-owned bank quietly did more to build Black DC's homeownership and wealth than the city's dominant financial institution.
