# A Howard Scientist Won the NAACP's Very First Medal

- Date: 2026-11-16
- Status: Posted
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1910s
- Place: Howard University, Northwest DC
- Tags: science, howard-university, naacp, black-history, biology, racial-discrimination

## Fact
In 1915 the NAACP's brand-new Spingarn Medal—its highest honor—went not to a politician or orator but to a cell biologist: Howard University's Ernest Everett Just, whose fertilization research reshaped embryology even as U.S. universities refused to hire him because of his race.

## Takeaway
Washington's Black intellectual community crowned a scientist as its first national hero, long before mainstream American science was willing to credit his work.
