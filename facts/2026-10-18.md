## Fact
Theodore Roosevelt Island, today a quiet nature preserve, was once Camp Greene, where the 1st U.S. Colored Troops trained before it became a desperately overcrowded camp housing up to 1,200 formerly enslaved people seeking freedom and work in the capital. Hundreds died there from malnutrition and disease.

## Takeaway
A beloved DC nature escape hides one of the city's most overlooked Civil War-era Black refugee sites.
