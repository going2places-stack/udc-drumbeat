## Fact
In March 1988, Gallaudet University students in Northeast DC locked the gates and shut down campus for eight days after trustees picked a hearing candidate as president over two qualified Deaf finalists. The protest, called Deaf President Now, forced the board to install the university's first Deaf president in its 124-year history.

## Takeaway
A student uprising on a quiet DC campus helped push disability rights into the national conversation and set the stage for the Americans with Disabilities Act two years later.
