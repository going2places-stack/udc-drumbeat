# DC's First Chinatown Was Razed to Build Federal Offices

- Date: 2026-10-14
- Status: Posted
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1920s-1930s
- Place: Chinatown / Federal Triangle
- Tags: chinatown, immigrant-history, displacement, federal-triangle, urban-renewal

## Fact
Washington's original Chinatown sat along Pennsylvania Avenue near the Capitol, full of Chinese-run restaurants, laundries, and grocery stores. In the late 1920s the government demolished the entire district to build what became the Federal Triangle complex, forcing merchants to relocate and build an entirely new Chinatown around H Street NW.

## Takeaway
A thriving immigrant business district was erased by federal development decades before the more famous 1980s convention-center displacement, showing the pattern repeats.
