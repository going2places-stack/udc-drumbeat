# DC's First 'Zoo' Was a Bison Pen on the Mall

## Fact
Before Rock Creek Park ever had a National Zoo, two live American bison grazed in a wooden paddock on the National Mall, penned behind the Smithsonian Castle in the late 1880s. The display, meant to save a vanishing species, drew crowds and within a few years grew into the National Zoological Park.

## Takeaway
A crisis-driven conservation stunt on the Mall quietly became the model for American wildlife institutions nationwide.
