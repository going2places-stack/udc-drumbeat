## Fact
When WMATA planned to bulldoze DC's oldest synagogue building for its new Metro headquarters in the 1960s, Jewish historians couldn't stop the wrecking ball with petitions alone. It took an act of Congress and President Nixon's own signature to legally save the 1876 Adas Israel building — which has since been relocated three separate times just to keep standing.

## Takeaway
A piece of Washington's immigrant religious history only survived because federal law itself had to be rewritten to protect it from the city's own transit construction.
