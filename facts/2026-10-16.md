# A SW DC Hardware Store Owner Rewrote U.S. Property Law

- Date: 2026-10-16
- Status: Posted

## Fact
When the government moved to bulldoze her non-blighted Fourth Street hardware store for 1950s urban renewal, Goldie Schneider sued, joined by a neighboring department store owner. Their case, Berman v. Parker, reached the U.S. Supreme Court, which ruled unanimously that governments could seize private property and hand it to private developers for redevelopment.

## Takeaway
A single DC shop owner's lost fight became the Supreme Court precedent that still underpins eminent domain and urban renewal nationwide.
