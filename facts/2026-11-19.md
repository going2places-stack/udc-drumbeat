# DC's Scrap Dealer Built — Then Sold Off — the Inaugural Stage

## Fact
Sidney Hechinger, the Southwest DC salvage dealer behind the future Hechinger hardware chain, supplied the lumber for the presidential inaugural platform in front of the Capitol in 1933, then dismantled the stand himself and resold cut pieces of the wood as souvenirs. He later provided lumber for Truman's and Eisenhower's inaugural stands too.

## Takeaway
One DC wrecking-and-salvage businessman literally recycled the physical stage of presidential power, turning a civic ritual into local commerce.
