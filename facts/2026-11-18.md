# Free Black Women Bought Their Own Burial Ground in 1842

- Date: 2026-11-18
- Status: Posted
- Angle: DC UNTOLD
- Era: 1840s
- Place: Georgetown
- Tags: black-women, land-ownership, antebellum, cemetery, women-led, underground-railroad

## Fact
In Georgetown, a mutual-aid group of free Black women called the Female Union Band Society pooled their money in 1842 to purchase land for a secular cemetery, guaranteeing dignified burial for members regardless of poverty, illness, or church affiliation.

## Takeaway
It's rare physical proof that Black women owned land and ran their own care institutions decades before emancipation.
